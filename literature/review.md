## Key Gaps This Paper Fills

1. **Transient MR design space:** No paper systematically addresses MR specifically for brief, transitional encounters (not sustained use)
2. **Non-HMD in social contexts:** Existing non-HMD work is technically focused; social/experiential aspects of non-HMD in public underexplored
3. **Form factor comparison for transient use:** No comparative study of handheld vs. neck-worn vs. stationary MR from a transient-use perspective
4. **Designer knowledge on transient MR:** The annotated portfolio method surfaces tacit design knowledge not captured elsewhere

## Recommended New References to Find

- Recent IMWUT papers (2022–2025) on mobile AR field deployments
- ISWC 2022–2025 papers on novel wearable form factors (especially non-standard mounting points)
- CHI papers on exhibition/museum interactive tech (especially post-COVID)
- Papers by Florian "Floyd" Mueller on embodied/somaesthetic computing (relevant to design philosophy)
