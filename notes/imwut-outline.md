### 6. Discussion (~2,500 words)
- 6.1 **Design Principles for Temporal Wearing:**

  - P2: Graceful Doff — exit feels natural
  - P3: Social Legibility — bystanders understand the brief interaction
  - P4: Invitation to Return — content rewards re-engagement
  - P5: Shared Glimpsing — enable social interaction around the moment
- 6.2 Temporal wearing beyond MR — IoT, health, accessibility
- 6.3 Designing for cumulative glimpses — content structure for repeated brief encounters
