### 2. Related Work (~2,500 words)

**2.1 Transient Mixed Reality**
- Most public MR is exhibition/museum/demo — inherently brief
- Devices designed for sustained use → discomfort, social awkwardness
- Social acceptability (Koelle, Profita) — HMDs in public = stigma
- The mismatch: transient contexts forced into session-wear devices

**2.2 Temporal Wearing & Wearability**
- Wearability frameworks (Gemperle, Zeagler) — WHERE/HOW but assume persistent wear
- Optical form factors through history: lorgnette, monocle, binoculars, viewfinder — all temporal
- Wearing duration & modality (Khurana, Park) — detachable modes
- Don/doff as cost vs don/doff as interaction
- Gap: wearability ignores the temporal dimension

**2.3 Transitional Interaction as Process**
- Not a single moment but a cycle: attract → approach → don → glimpse → doff → rest → return
- Calm technology (Weiser & Brown) — center/periphery transitions
- Peripheral interaction (Bakker) — attention shifts
- Transitional interfaces (Hubenschmid, Ens) — cross-device, cross-modality
- Micro-mobility (Luff & Heath) — physical manipulation in social space
- Gap: existing transition work is linear (A→B), not cyclic

**2.8 Summary: The Gap**
- (1) MR increasingly used in transient public contexts
- (2) Optical wearing has always been temporal but never theorized
- (3) The interaction is a repeating cycle, not a single session
- → Temporal wearing unifies all three

### 6. Discussion (~2,500 words)
- 6.1 **Temporal Wearing as MR Paradigm** — what this means for the field; challenging "longer is better"; beyond MR (IoT, health, accessibility)
- 6.2 **Design Principles for Temporal Wearing:**
  - P1: Designing for Discovery — device appearance invites curiosity; how people notice there's a hidden world
  - P2: Instant-On — transition cost < 2 seconds
  - P3: Graceful Doff — exit feels natural
  - P4: Social Legibility — bystanders understand the interaction
  - P5: Invitation to Return — content rewards re-engagement, not penalizes brevity
  - P6: Shared Glimpsing — enable social interaction around the moment
