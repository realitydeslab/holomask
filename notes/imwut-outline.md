# Just a Glimpse: Temporal Wearing as a Design Paradigm for Transient Mixed Reality

## IMWUT Paper Outline — Target: May 1, 2026

---

### 1. Introduction (~1,500 words)
- 1.1 Opening vignette: a museum visitor picks up a handheld MR viewer, glimpses a virtual artifact for 3 seconds, puts it down — then comes back
- 1.2 Problem: wearable MR assumes sustained session-based use; donning/doffing = friction
- 1.3 Observation: brief MR encounters already exist and thrive but lack theoretical grounding
- 1.4 Our concept: **temporal wearing** — wearing IS the interaction, measured in seconds
- 1.5 Contributions:
  - (C1) The concept of temporal wearing and a three-part wearing duration taxonomy
  - (C2) A design space for temporal wearable MR (body coupling × transition cost × social legibility)
  - (C3) Six MR prototypes instantiating temporal wearing across form factors
  - (C4) Empirical findings from field deployment
  - (C5) Five design principles for temporal wearing

### 2. Related Work (~2,500 words)
- 2.1 Wearability Frameworks — Gemperle, Zeagler, Starner → WHERE/HOW but not FOR HOW LONG
- 2.2 Wearing Duration & Modality — Khurana (detachable smartwatch), Park → closest, no duration taxonomy

### 7. Findings (~2,500 words)
- 7.1 Transition metrics per prototype
- 7.2 Re-engagement patterns — **key: lower transition cost → more re-engagements; shorter wear = more total MR exposure**
- 7.3 Social behaviors — shared glimpsing, social MR without isolation
- 7.4 Social comfort — survey results across form factors
- 7.5 Emergent temporal wearing behaviors:
  - "The double-take" — quick glimpse then return
  - "The hand-off" — passing to companion
  - "The narrator" — one wears, describes to others
  - "The orbit" — circling back during visit
- 7.6 Interview themes

### 8. Design Principles for Temporal Wearing (~1,500 words)
- P1: **Instant-On** — transition cost < 2 seconds
- P2: **Graceful Doff** — putting down feels natural, not like abandoning
- P3: **Social Legibility** — bystanders instantly understand the brief interaction
- P4: **Invitation to Return** — content rewards re-engagement, not penalizes brevity
- P5: **Shared Glimpsing** — enable social interaction around the temporal wearing moment
