# Venue Analysis: UbiComp/IMWUT vs ISWC
*For: "Just a Glimpse: Designing Seamless Transitional Interfaces for Transient Mixed Reality Use"*

## 1. Venue Overviews

### IMWUT (UbiComp Journal)

**Publication model:** Rolling journal, 4 issues/year. Papers in IMWUT 2025 Issue 4 and IMWUT 2026 Issues 1–3 are invited to present at UbiComp/ISWC 2026 (Shanghai, Oct 11–15).

**Deadlines:**
- Feb 1 → June issue
- **May 1** → September issue (to appear at UbiComp 2026)
- Aug 1 → December issue
- Nov 1 → March issue

**Typical papers:** System + user evaluation; field deployments; large-scale behavioral studies; design explorations with empirical validation; novel interaction techniques + user studies

**Methodology:** Empirical rigor expected. RtD alone is rare—usually paired with formative/summative evaluation. Typical N≥12–20. Full journal paper: 25–35 pages.

---

### ISWC 2026 — Notes and Briefs Track

**Deadline: May 17, 2026 [AoE]**
**Conference: October 11–15, 2026, Shanghai, China**
**Notification: July 1, 2026 | Camera ready: July 24, 2026**
**Format: 4–6 pages (ACM 2-column)**

**Focus areas:**
- Novel Devices and Form Factors (glasses, earables, rings, neck-worn)
- Enabling Technologies: wearable AR optics, haptics, displays
- Design Process for Wearables (explicitly includes RtD, craft & computing)
- Culturally-sensitive and conceptual design innovation
- On-skin/close-body interfaces
- Social and experiential implications

**Methodology:** Much more flexible. Design explorations and early-stage systems accepted. Notes/Briefs suit focused contributions. Expert evaluation or brief user study encouraged but not mandatory.

## 2. DIS vs UbiComp/ISWC Differences

| Dimension | DIS | IMWUT | ISWC Notes |
|-----------|-----|-------|-----------|
| RtD / annotated portfolios | Highly valued | Rare; needs validation | Explicitly accepted |
| User studies | Optional | Usually required (N≥12) | Encouraged, not mandatory |
| Prototype fidelity | Low-fi OK | Working system required | Early-stage OK |
| Theory contribution | Strong | Weaker | Weaker |
| Page length | 6–15 | 25–35 | 4–6 |

## 3. Reframing Needed

### For IMWUT:
- Add empirical evaluation with real users (museum/exhibition deployment)
- Reframe from "design knowledge" to "design + evaluation findings"
- Metrics: glance duration, transition time, social acceptability, preference
- New hook: "Transient MR interaction" as measurable technical category

### For ISWC:
- Keep: design-led framing, 6 prototypes, annotated portfolio insights
- Add: brief expert design walkthrough (N=5–8 suffices for Notes)
- Reframe: as novel wearable form factors for transient AR contexts
- Emphasize: neck-worn + body-worn as new wearable sub-categories
- Compress: 6 prototypes → 3–4 key exemplars
- New hook: Design space for transient wearable MR beyond HMDs
